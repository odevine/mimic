NDPMTG is a Magic: The Gathering symbol font by Nick DePaola (ndepaola on
GitHub, https://github.com/ndepaola). It is used across community proxy
rendering projects and is bundled here for a non-commercial project that is
labeled as such.

The font software ships no license notice of its own. The mana, tap, and card
type symbols it depicts are copyright Wizards of the Coast; the glyph outlines
are the author's work. This project claims no affiliation with, nor endorsement
by, Wizards of the Coast.

If you are the author and want this attribution changed or the font removed,
that will be honored.
